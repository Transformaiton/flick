## Documentation

Issues and pull requests about the customer guide are welcome. Keep the guide in plain English for a small-business reader, and keep prices tied to the vendor page they came from.

## Showcase examples

A showcase example made with Flick can be added under `examples/<your-example-name>/` with:

- the rendered animation or a link to it
- a short `README.md` with a title and a one-line description
- the input type (`video`, `transcript`, or `script`) and the aspect ratio
- confirmation that you have permission to share every asset in the example

Leave out private client material, secrets, and assets you do not have the right to distribute.
